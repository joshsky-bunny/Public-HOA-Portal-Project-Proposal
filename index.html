01 / EFFICIENCY

Zero cost to the HOA

Our team will analyze, design, build, and test the system free of charge as part of our major academic requirements.

02 / CLARITY

Time back for the board

Automate billing records and payment tracking instead of manually cutting, printing, and distributing paper bills.

03 / TRUST

An audit-ready record

Every edit and financial record view is logged, creating transparency and helping prevent unauthorized data leaks.

One system, two experiences

Everything in its right place.

The entire picture is visible to the board. Only what is theirs is visible to each resident.

For the board & treasurer

Run the neighborhood with confidence.

  • Manage resident records in one organized dashboard
  • Generate monthly billing statement records
  • Track collections and verify digital payments
  • Review a complete administrator audit trail
For residents

Know where you stand, anytime.

  • Log in securely to a private individual account
  • View outstanding balances and billing history
  • Upload GCash or bank transfer receipts
  • Read official community announcements
Privacy is a foundation

Built with RA 10173 in mind.

Resident rosters and financial dues deserve more than loose papers or unprotected spreadsheets. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) keeps private data private, so residents can never see another household's account or financial standing.
